# Sprint 1 Week 2 – MVP Requirements

## New direction
The original Week 2 checklist was based on further developing and comparing the use cases. Prior to the change in direction, initial work had been completed on the use cases, including identifying their problems, potential users and value. However, the remaining detailed assumptions, dependencies, acceptance measures and comparison work were not progressed further once the project priority shifted towards supporting the Week 3 prototype.

Following the client discussion, the immediate priority shifted towards developing something tangible that could be demonstrated in Week 3. As a result, creating a detailed candidate requirements pack and a completed comparison matrix was deprioritised at this stage.

## Week 3 Prototype Objective
The objective for Week 3 is to produce a demonstrable visual output representing the digital human concept.

The intended prototype should display a digital human where technically feasible. If this cannot be implemented within the available timeframe, a suitable placeholder may be used to demonstrate progress. Visible mouth movement or lip synchronisation may also be included where feasible but is not required for the minimum Week 3 outcome.

For the Week 3 demonstration, the prototype may use fixed or pre-recorded speech audio rather than requiring real-time AI interaction.

The minimum Week 3 outcome is:
Fixed/ Pre-recorded Speech Audio -> Digital Human or Placeholder -> Visual and Audio Output

The Week 3 prototype is intended to establish a working foundation.

## MVP Requirements
| ID | Requirements | Priority |
| -- | -------------| -------- |
| R-01 | The prototype shall produce a demonstrable visual output representing the digital human concept. | High |
| R-02 | The prototype shall support fixed or pre-recorded speech audio for the Week 3 demonstration. | High |
| R-03 | The prototype should display a digital human where technically feasible. | Moderate |
| R-04 | The prototype may support visible mouth movement or lip synchronisation where technically feasible. | Low |

## Technical Investigation
The technologies identified during Week 2 are potential options and are not confirmed as the final technical architecture.

1. uLipSync - A potential option for supporting lip synchronisation. If it is compatible with the development environment, it may be used to demonstrate visible mouth movement in the Week 3 prototype or later. As this functionality still requires technical validation, lip synchronisation is treated as an optional rather than a minimum Week 3 requirement.

2. Unity-QuestConversationalAI - A potential option for future real-time conversational functionality. This is not currently considered a requirement for the Week 3 demonstration.

3. Hugging Face - A potential option for future AI speech and conversational functionality. Further technical testing is required to confirm compatibility and suitability for the project.

## Dependencies and Constraints
- Development Time - The team has a limited timeframe to produce something demonstrable for Week 3. The prototype is therefore scoped around a basic visual and audio demonstration, with additional functionality included only where technically feasible.

- Digital Human - The digital human may require additional setup or configuration. If it cannot be completed within the available timeframe, a suitable placeholder may be used for the Week 3 demonstration.

- Lip Synchronisation - Lip synchronisation depends on compatibility. As this has not yet been validated and may not be achievable within the available timeframe, it is not essential to the minimum Week 3 outcome.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The prototype must display a visual output representing the digital human concept.
- The prototype must support fixed or pre-recorded speech audio when running.
